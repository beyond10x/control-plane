# From workspace goal to a visible, verified outcome

Control plane is a live operator application for autonomous engineering across multiple workspaces. Its product vision is docs/vision.md. The operator sees actual planner and worker activity, recent progress, system freshness and actionable blockers, and can pause, cancel or revise goals without losing context.

Autonomous ESS-first AEP planning, isolated implementation, independent review and observed authorized publication remain the delivery path. A configuration surface with an opaque Planning label does not satisfy the product. Live visibility and the control-plane operating experience are required alongside backend correctness.

Operator direction, 2026-10-05: the application must show live ticks, status and progress; update the vision and delivery agenda. Existing Rust, foundation-only, ESS, AEP, worktree and bot-authority constraints remain in force.
