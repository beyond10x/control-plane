Wave 13 approved on 2026-10-14 under the operator's delegated decision authority: waves are approved up front, proposed on their wave page and run without waiting for an answer; one wave, one integration branch, one pull request, merged once its checks pass.

The decision: move control-plane onto a released Loom now, without waiting for an ESS release. Control-plane is the integration repository where Loom's foundation for consumers is proved; consumers read Loom as its released crates and as the `b10x-loom` command, whose `run --output jsonl` events are a stable contract.

## Units

1. story:loom-release-pin: the `b10x-loom-sdk` pin moves from commit 1b25fe89 to the newest Loom release tag; every crossed break is adapted and mapped to its CHANGELOG entry; a break the CHANGELOG does not name is a defect in Loom, reported to Loom.
2. story:loom-pin-foundation-checks: the foundation check refuses a Loom pin that is not a tag, and a Loom crate resolved twice.

The two touch separate files and run at once.

## Conditions

- story:admission-conformance-target and story:spec-owned-busy-rules stay out while their ESS upstream-blockers are open; stories that need a real-model run stay out. No real-model run.
- Spec first: a crossed break that changes a projected contract is modelled in `ess/` before code.
- The newest ess (0.59.0) runs from its archive in a scratch directory; nothing is installed machine-wide.
- Every build and test runs under the host's disk reservation; load over 30 means `CARGO_BUILD_JOBS=8`.
- Package gates of every touched crate on the exact commit before each push; the pull request's CI is the full gate.
- No release (the repository has no release process).
